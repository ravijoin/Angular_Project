Information

Once you place an order, an approval request will be sent to the admin. If approved, your order will be processed.

You can order a maximum of 3 medicines that have not yet been picked up.

Expired medicines can be exchanged after one year.

If medicines are not picked up after 10 days, you will receive an email reminder.

A fine of 50 Rs per day will be imposed for medicines not picked up after 10 days. You will be notified by email.

If the total fine exceeds 500 Rs or the medicine is damaged, your account may be blocked.
