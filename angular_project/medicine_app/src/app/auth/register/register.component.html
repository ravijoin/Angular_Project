Information

Once you place an order, an approval request will be sent to the admin. If that is approved, your order will be processed.

You can have a maximum of 3 medicines ordered that have not been picked up.

You can exchange a medicine after 1 year if expired.

If a medicine is not picked up after 10 days, you will receive an email reminder.

After the 10th day, a fine of 50 Rs will be imposed every day for each medicine that has not been picked up. This fine will be communicated to you via email daily.

If your total fine exceeds 500 Rs or if the medicine is damaged, the admin can block your account.
